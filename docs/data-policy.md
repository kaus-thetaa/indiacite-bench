# Data policy

- No redistribution of judgment text beyond excerpts of 600 characters at most.
- Real incidents are anonymised. Link to the decision instead of copying it.
- Keep attribution for the incident database on every derived record.
- Respect the terms of every legal database used. Record the date the terms were read.
- No API keys, tokens or private documents in the repository.
- The benchmark measures tools. It must not be presented as proving that a citation is correct.
- Annotators are told what the data is used for. No personal data beyond what is in the public decision, and none in released excerpts.
